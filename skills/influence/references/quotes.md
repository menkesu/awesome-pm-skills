# Influence: Verified Quotes

## Influence vs politics, and the exec as your user
- "Politics is manipulating outcomes and people for your own gain. Influence is about increasing the odds that your good ideas survive." — Jessica Fain, Lenny's Podcast (00:13:24)
- "if we can take some of those skills of building great products and think about our executive as our key user here, then we can have a much, much more productive conversation." — Jessica Fain, Lenny's Podcast (00:11:36)
- "The other thing people don't realize is execs want to be successful too. They want to be good at their jobs. And how can you help them?" — Jessica Fain, Lenny's Podcast (00:29:22)

## Requests, trade-offs, managing up
- "if you're doing product management really well, you never have to say yes and you never have to say no. You're giving people options and you're helping them understand the trade-offs." — Matt LeMay, Lenny's Podcast (01:08:24)
- "You don't tell your boss no, you ask your boss to help you prioritize. That is your boss' most important job is to prioritize." — Nir Eyal, Lenny's Podcast (01:06:40)
- "Give them a menu of items. And you'll see that you're able to execute much, much faster on four things rather than eight at the same time." — Geoff Charles, Lenny's Podcast (00:26:56)
- "say you're going to do the thing, say that you're doing the thing, and then say that you did it." — Peter Deng, Lenny's Podcast (01:19:30)

- "it's critical to ask what kind of experience am I? That is where to start? Not how good am I at my job? How much do I know? How critical am I to this process?" — Evan LaPointe, Lenny's Podcast (01:32:16)
