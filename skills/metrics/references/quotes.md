## Choosing the north star
- "When a user completes this action, it's clear that they both understand the utility of the product, they understand what that product is all about, and it's an action that, if they perform the action, they're very likely to come back." — Sarah Tavel, Lenny's Podcast (00:05:45)
- "you know you've got something really right with the core action when it's helping both sides of your network." — Sarah Tavel, Lenny's Podcast (00:37:10)
- "customers don't care how big you are, they don't care how many transactions you've accumulated. What they care about is when they have a transaction with you, how happy do you make them?" — Sarah Tavel, Lenny's Podcast (00:50:11)
- "if the race that you think you're running is to grow MAUs or to grow GMV, you're not actually going to run the right race." — Sarah Tavel, Lenny's Podcast (00:39:42)
- "the North Star metric measures how much value we create for the market." — Itamar Gilad, Lenny's Podcast (00:27:02)
- "So units of value from the customer perspective I think is more important than overall revenue." — Sean Ellis, Lenny's Podcast (01:21:17)
- "having stability in north star metric for at least six months feels like table stakes to me and that's for the reasons that I cited before, for building momentum, for building domain expertise, and also for compounding impact" — Lauryn Isford, Lenny's Podcast (00:36:36)
- "I think the majority of the value is in the latter, is just having extreme clarity around the goal and that allowed everybody to work towards optimizing the same goal." — Naomi Gleit, Lenny's Podcast (00:34:19)
- "how many people turn to Arc at least five days a week? That is all we obsess over from a metrics perspective, because for us it captures retention, engagement, and growth in a single metric." — Josh Miller, Lenny's Podcast (00:05:15)
