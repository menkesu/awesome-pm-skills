## Free tier and conversion

- "If it gets you to the aha moment, that path to habit formation, that has to be free. That's the core utility of your product." — Naomi Ionita, Lenny's Podcast (12:44)
- "you're inadvertently cheapening your product. People attribute a lower dollar value or a $0 value to what you've built." — Naomi Ionita, Lenny's Podcast (09:47)
- "In free to pay conversion, there's three pillars that you constantly need to work on, and one pillar dominates them all. It's monetization awareness." — Elena Verna, Lenny's Podcast (01:00:56)
- "personally I'm in the camp of offering a reverse trial. Funky name, but what that means is offer a trial but also offer freemium. Do both." — Lauryn Isford, Lenny's Podcast (00:39:14)

## Raising and holding price
- "So what often happens is you raise prices and signups don't change." — Jason Cohen, Lenny's Podcast (00:37:16)
- "The reason is that pricing selects the market." — Jason Cohen, Lenny's Podcast (00:38:56)
- "if your NPS is over 20, which is not a very high NPS, you should raise your prices once per year." — Patrick Campbell, Lenny's Podcast (00:22:30)
- "your reluctance to do a price increase is often internal and emotional and it's not external and logical." — Madhavan Ramanujam, Lenny's Podcast (00:55:58)
- "If you just discounted price, guess what's going to happen six months later, that's going to be your new price." — Madhavan Ramanujam, Lenny's Podcast (01:28:46)
- "If you raised prices 8% last year, did you add 30% more value to your product?" — Jason Lemkin, Lenny's Podcast (01:35:21)

## Enterprise and cadence
- "If your win rate is higher than that, your price is too low." — Jen Abel, Lenny's Podcast (01:08:41)
- "discounting just to get the deal over the line, you're negotiating with yourself." — Jen Abel, Lenny's Podcast (01:00:54)
- "find one thing you're going to do every three months, put a calendar invite, let it renew every three months." — Patrick Campbell, Lenny's Podcast (00:19:42)
