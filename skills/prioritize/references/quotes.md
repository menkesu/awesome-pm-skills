- "The value isn't in your roadmap, the value is in the roadmapping process." — Janna Bastow, Lenny's Podcast (00:47)
- "It just means not penalizing ourselves by having a date on everything on the roadmap." — Janna Bastow, Lenny's Podcast (26:18)
- "I recommend using outcome roadmaps saying by October we want to achieve this outcome." — Itamar Gilad, Lenny's Podcast (01:02:54)
- "it felt like once it was on the roadmap, it had to be done, and that's just not smart." — Paige Costello, Lenny's Podcast (00:10:38)
- "the first three months of that roadmap, we have a 80% position level, which means that 80% of the things that we claim to be on the roadmap will get done." — Varun Parmar, Lenny's Podcast (00:47:43)

- "And then all of a sudden you've got 90% of your resources committed to things that don't track against your strategy." — Maggie Crowley, Lenny's Podcast (00:47:37)

## Goals and OKRs
- "So, I say OKRs are more of a vitamin, they're not a medicine." — Christina Wodtke, Lenny's Podcast (00:05:29)
- "What am I doing this week to get to the outcome I really want?" — Christina Wodtke, Lenny's Podcast (00:14:46)
- "So, making sure that you have real outcomes that let you move forward, I think that's the biggest mistake people make in OKRs." — Christina Wodtke, Lenny's Podcast (00:25:09)
- "My feeling is, a good goal is one that makes you feel somewhat uncomfortable but not doomed." — Christina Wodtke, Lenny's Podcast (00:54:07)
- "no company needs more than three company goals and the point of company goals is to help people know what the most important things are to success." — Molly Graham, Lenny's Podcast (00:47:52)
- "two people owning a goal is no one owning a goal. One person owns the goal, who is it?" — Molly Graham, Lenny's Podcast (00:52:09)
- "if you don't understand how to move a particular metric, then the right goal is to set a goal to increase your understanding not to move that metric." — Ravi Mehta, Lenny's Podcast (00:49:27)
- "I would rather have all the OKRs be red or yellow and we missed everything and we learned around why we missed it than everything to be green." — Jiaona Zhang, Lenny's Podcast (00:32:29)
- "And that leads to what I call toddler soccer, where everybody just runs to the same surface or the same customer set or the same exact product where you can do this and it's measurable" — Ami Vora, Lenny's Podcast (00:56:27)
